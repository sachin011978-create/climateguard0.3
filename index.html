🌡️ Extreme Heat Survival

Stay alive when temperatures soar above 40°C

⚠️ Heat stroke can kill in under 15 minutes at 45°C+ — act before symptoms appear
Survival Essentials
💧
Hydrate aggressively
2–4 cups of water every hour. Add electrolytes. Avoid caffeine, alcohol, and sugary drinks.
🏠
Create cool zones
Block sunlight with thick curtains. Wet sheets on windows. Lowest floor stays coolest.
🧊
Cool your core
Ice packs on neck, wrists, ankles. A cool shower every 2 hours reduces core temperature.
👕
Dress for survival
Loose, light-colored, breathable cotton. Wide-brimmed hat and UV sunglasses when outdoors.
Emergency Steps if Someone Collapses
1
Move to shade or A/C immediately. Never leave them alone. Call 108 (India emergency).
2
Cool rapidly — cold wet cloths, ice packs on neck, armpits, and groin area.
3
Do NOT give fluids to unconscious person. Fan them, remove excess clothing, wait for emergency services.

🌊 Flash Flood Survival

Every second counts when water rises

⚡ Floods can rise 3 metres in minutes. Never wait to evacuate when warned.
Immediate Actions
⬆️
Get to high ground now
Don't wait for water to stop rising. Move to the highest nearby ground immediately.
🚗
Abandon vehicles
30cm of moving water sweeps a car. Exit immediately and climb to higher ground.
⚡
Avoid electricity
Floodwater carries current. Stay far from panels, wires, and downed poles.
📻
Monitor IMD alerts
Battery radio. Follow every evacuation order. Never return until "all clear" is issued.
Preparation Steps
1
Know your flood zone. Map nearest high ground and two evacuation routes from home.
2
Pack a waterproof go-bag with 3 days of food, water, medicines, torch, and vital documents.
3
Move valuables upstairs and raise electrical equipment off the ground floor before monsoon.

⛈️ Heavy Rainfall Safety

Monsoon & extreme rain survival for South Asia

🌧️ Waterlogged roads hide open manholes. Do not go out unless absolutely necessary.
Safety During Heavy Rain
🏠
Stay indoors
Flooded roads hide manholes and potholes. Even knee-deep moving water can knock you over.
⛰️
Avoid hillsides
Prolonged rain saturates soil — landslides occur without warning in hilly and coastal areas.
🌳
Never under trees
Lightning strike + falling branches. A concrete structure is always safer during storms.
📱
Charge everything
Keep devices at 100%. Download offline maps. Save numbers: 100 (police), 101 (fire), 108 (ambulance).
If Caught Outdoors
1
Find solid concrete shelter — not a canopy or tin shed. Lower your profile in open spaces.
2
Never walk in flowing water — fast-moving shin-deep water creates enough force to knock an adult down.
3
Alert family of your location before conditions worsen. Call early, not when already in danger.

🌀 Hurricane & Cyclone Survival

Preparation is the difference between life and death

🚨 Category 3+ storms bring 180+ km/h winds and 4–6m storm surges. Evacuate coastal areas immediately.
72-Hour Preparation Window
🪟
Board up windows
Plywood or storm shutters only. Tape is a dangerous myth — it does not prevent shattering.
🍱
Stock 7 days of supplies
Non-perishable food, 1 gallon water per person per day, medications and full first-aid kit.
🔋
Power backup
Charged power banks, LED torches, battery radio, and a fueled generator stored outdoors.
📄
Secure documents
Aadhaar, insurance, medical records in a sealed waterproof bag. Know 2 evacuation routes.
During the Storm
1
Stay in interior rooms, away from all windows. Lowest safe floor unless flooding is expected.
2
Never go outside in the eye. The calm is temporary — the back wall is often even stronger.
3
Stay off roads 24+ hours after. Downed power lines are invisible beneath floodwater.

🎒 Emergency Survival Kit

Every family needs this ready before disaster strikes

✅ Build your kit before emergency season. 72 hours of self-sufficiency is the standard goal.
Essential Items
💧
Water
4 litres per person per day. Purification tablets and a filter bottle as backup.
🥫
Food
Non-perishable: canned goods, dry biscuits, trail mix, instant oats — minimum 3 days.
💊
Medical
First aid kit, all prescriptions (30-day supply), ORS packets, thermometer, BP monitor.
🔦
Power & light
LED torch + batteries, 20,000mAh power bank, battery-powered radio for alerts.
📄
Documents
Aadhaar, passport, insurance, bank details in a sealed waterproof zip-lock bag.
🧰
Tools & extras
Whistle, multi-tool, N95 masks, waterproof poncho, cash in small denominations.
